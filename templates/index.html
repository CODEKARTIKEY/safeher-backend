08 / SAFETY GUIDE

Small habits. Stronger safety.

Practical reminders for everyday situations.

01Walking alone

Prefer well-lit routes, keep your phone accessible and share your expected arrival time.

02Ride safety

Verify the vehicle and driver, share your trip and avoid unnecessary personal details.

03Digital safety

Use strong passwords, device lock, two-factor authentication and review permissions.

04Home safety

Keep emergency numbers accessible and ensure trusted contacts can reach you.

05Stay connected

Use check-ins when travelling late or meeting someone new.

06Trust your instincts

If a situation feels wrong, move toward a public place and seek help.
